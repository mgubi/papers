A simple construction of the sine-Gordon model via stochastic quantization

Massimiliano Gubinelli, Martin Hairer, Tadahiro Oh and Younes Zine

First issued 20 December 2024 · PDF

Abstract

We present a simple PDE construction of the sine-Gordon measure below the first threshold (${\beta}^2 < 4\pi$), in both the finite and infinite volume settings, by studying the corresponding parabolic sine-Gordon model. We also establish pathwise global well-posedness of the hyperbolic sine-Gordon model in finite volume for ${\beta}^2 < 2\pi$.
